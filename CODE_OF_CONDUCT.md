# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want FootballVision to be a welcoming, constructive project for people of
all backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors, maintainers, and users are
expected to treat one another with respect in every project space.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, useful technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas and tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and consent when sharing match data or examples.
- Accept correction gracefully and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Help make the project accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious
violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

For a sensitive conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately using
the contact method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/heetdalsania).
Do not use a public issue if doing so would expose personal or sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where
practical, and avoid conflicts of interest when deciding on enforcement.
